When I joined Etsy, my first project was to speed up experimentation through variance reduction, migrating our CUPED implementation to support CUPAC.

I wrote a post in Etsy's engineering blog that shares how I shortened the average experiment duration by 3 days from implementing CUPAC at scale, as well as the learnings along the way. 

Check out the [blog post](https://www.etsy.com/ca/codeascraft/reducing-experiment-duration-with-predicted-control-variates).
