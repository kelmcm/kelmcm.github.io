title: Starting Recurse Center
date: 2026-11-04
excerpt: Why I'm doing a coding retreat.  
---

“Finally, back to civilization.”

This is what I thought on a Saturday morning in August after emerging from a 3-day, 4-night canoe-camping trip in Algonquin Park. That last morning was the hardest. A 2.4 km portage and a sporadic summer thunder and lighting storm forced us to seek refuge at someone else’s campsite for 1 hour. It had already been a hard day.

As my phone slowly reconnected to the cell network, I started receiving a number of messages, calls, Whatapp texts all from… my coworkers?

As I started reading the cryptic messages “Are you okay?”, “Are you safe?”, “I’m so sorry this happened.”, I realized what was going on. I was laid off.

Suddenly, I had free time to do whatever I wanted. An amazing, but scary thought. I could look for a new job, visit family, travel… anything.

One night, I received a simple message from a colleague: “BTW - Have you heard of Recurse Center?”. I had not, but after a quick peruse of the website, I had and I was invested.

This week, I started the self directed, programming retreat at [Recurse Center](https://www.recurse.com/) (RC, for short). I’m joining a cohort of 30 other programmers from different backgrounds, career points with varying skills. The goal here is to learn. Learn by doing, code early and often, and pair-program whenever possible.

I decided to join Recurse to take a few months to learn a new area of data engineering and push myself to learn other aspects of engineering that I haven’t crossed paths with. Rust? Strudel? p5.js? Haven’t hear of them, but will try!

What interested me the most is the change in thinking the RC environment creates. Imagine growing up playing guitar and jamming with you friends. When you are older, you become a professional guitarist and have to play the same song over and over every night. RC is a jam sesh, but for coding. And, you have 30 new friends to jam with! It creates room for play and curiosity with no other reason.

I’m hoping to come out of RC with a fresh mind, new way of thinking, and refound joy for data and programming. It’s been 3 days, and I already see this happening.
